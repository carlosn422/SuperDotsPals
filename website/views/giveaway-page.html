Giveaway FAQ

Do I need to buy anything to enter?

No. A MoodPals account is free, and buying MoodPals Plus does not improve your chances of winning.

How do I enter?

Create a free account in the MoodPals app and confirm your email before entries close. Each registered account is entered once automatically. There is nothing to fill in on this website.

Do Plus members get extra entries?

No. Everyone gets one entry, and every eligible entry has the same chance to win.

What is the 1,000-member goal?

It's a community milestone we're celebrating together. It is not an entry requirement, and the drawing happens on the published dates whether or not we reach it. The count includes only verified paid Plus memberships.

How many times can I enter?

Once per person. Each registered account counts as one entry, and extra accounts made by the same person are not counted.

How will I know if I won?

We'll email the potential winners after the drawing. Winners must confirm eligibility within the time in the Official Rules, or an alternate may be chosen. We'll never ask you to pay to claim a prize.

Is Apple involved?

No. Apple is not a sponsor of, and is not involved in, this giveaway. Apple Watch is a trademark of Apple Inc.

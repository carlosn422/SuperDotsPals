Questions

Is the water and caffeine tracker free?

Yes. Logging water and caffeine on iPhone and Apple Watch is free, with a Day view. MoodPals Plus adds Week and Month views, a custom water goal and caffeine limit, and water reminders.

How much caffeine is too much?

The U.S. FDA says up to 400 mg a day is generally not associated with negative effects for most healthy adults. That's the default daily guide in MoodPals; with Plus you can set your own limit.

Can I log drinks from my Apple Watch?

Yes. On your own Apple Watch, tap the water drop in the top corner of the MoodPals app and tap a cup. Family (mood-only) watches don't show drink tracking.

Does coffee count toward hydration?

It can. When you log a caffeine drink, MoodPals can add its volume to your water total too.

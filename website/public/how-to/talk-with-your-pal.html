Talk with your pal

Pick a buddy, call, and talk about your day.

  1. 1

    Pick your buddy

    Go to More › Your buddy. Tap a pal to hear them say hi.

  2. 2

    Call and talk

    Tap Call. After a soft ring, just talk. Tap the red button to hang up.

  3. 3

    It lands in your diary

    Your pal saves the important part. Find it in Actions › Stress diary.

Plus and talk minutes

How MoodPals Plus and minute packs work.

  1. Get Plus

    Open More → MoodPals Plus. Weekly $6.99, Monthly $12.99 or Annual $39.99.

  2. 15 minutes a month

    Every plan includes 15 minutes of talk time each month.

  3. Need more?

    When you run out, the call screen offers 10, 30 or 60 minutes. Packs never expire.

  4. Manage

    Restore purchases on the Plus screen. Cancel in your Apple Account settings.

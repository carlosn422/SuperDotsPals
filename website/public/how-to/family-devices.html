Link a family iPad

Use an iPad or spare iPhone as a family device for your kids.

  1. On the iPad

    Install MoodPals and tap Link as a Family Device. A 6-digit code appears.

  2. On your iPhone

    Open Family → Family Devices → Add Device and enter the code. Confirm on the iPad.

  3. Choose apps

    On the iPad, allow Screen Time and choose the apps to pause.

  4. Start screen time

    On your iPhone, open a child in Family, pick the device and minutes, then Start.

  5. Lock from your wrist

    Lock, unlock, add time or end it from your iPhone or Apple Watch.

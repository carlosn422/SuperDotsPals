Family devices

Link a family iPad and run your child's screen time from your iPhone or watch.

  1. 1

    Get the code on the iPad

    Open MoodPals on the iPad and tap Use as a family device.

  2. 2

    Add the device

    On your iPhone: Family › Family Devices › Add Device, then enter the code.

  3. 3

    Start a timer

    Pick a child and a time. The iPad counts down and locks apps at zero.

  4. 4

    Run it from your wrist

    On Apple Watch, start a timer for your child, then tap Add Time or Stop.

  5. 5

    Lock from your wrist

    On Apple Watch, tap the lock to lock or unlock the device's apps.

Questions

Which apps can I track?

Any apps, categories or websites you choose with Apple's Screen Time picker, such as TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Snapchat or games.

How does the daily limit work?

You set one daily limit, for example 1 hour, and it is shared by all the apps you pick. When their time adds up to 1 hour that day, the limit screen appears. Tap Take a break to close the app, or I need it today to keep going until midnight. The limit resets every night.

Does MoodPals see what I do in my apps?

No. Apple Screen Time only tells MoodPals how long you used the apps you chose. It never shows messages, posts or what you watched, and your usage stays on your iPhone.

How is screen time connected to my stress?

MoodPals looks at your Apple Watch HRV stress readings in the two hours after you use each app and the moods you log, then compares them with your usual. After a few days you can see which apps tend to come before higher or lower stress. It's a pattern, not a diagnosis.

Is it free?

Screen Time & Stress and the daily limit are free for 14 days, a trial shared with Screen Break for kids. Then they're part of MoodPals Plus for $12.99 a month, $39.99 a year or $6.99 a week. You need an Apple Watch for the stress part.

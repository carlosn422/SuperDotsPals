Questions

Does MoodPals track sleep by itself?

MoodPals reads the sleep your Apple Watch records in Apple Health. Turn on Sleep in the Health app and wear your watch to bed, and MoodPals shows your time asleep, stages and heart rate while asleep. MoodPals only reads from Apple Health and never writes to it.

Is the sleep tracker free?

Yes. Free shows each night for the last 7 days. MoodPals Plus adds Week and Month views and 30 days of sleep and fitness history.

What if my iPhone hasn't synced with my watch?

Apple Health on your iPhone is always used first. If a night is missing there, MoodPals fills it in from your Apple Watch so your history keeps working.

How does sleep affect my stress?

Short or broken sleep often shows up as lower HRV and higher stress the next day. MoodPals shows your sleep next to your stress level and energy score, and Ask MoodPal (Plus) can explain the link using your own numbers.

Getting started

Do I need an account? No. Tap Get Started to use MoodPals as a guest. Create an account later in More → Settings to sync across devices or use My Circle; everything you saved comes with you.

Why is my stress score empty? MoodPals needs heart rate variability readings from an Apple Watch. Make sure Apple Health access is on (iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → MoodPals) and wear your watch. On the watch you can tap Measure now for a fresh reading.

Apple Watch

How do I put my mood on my watch face? Install MoodPals on your watch (iPhone Watch app → MoodPals → Install), then long-press your watch face, tap Edit, choose a complication slot and pick Mood, Mood & Stress or Stress score.

My complication looks out of date. Open the MoodPals watch app once, or remove the complication and add it again.

My Circle and sharing

What do friends see? Only the moods you choose to share, with the groups you pick. Never your health data or diary. Shared moods disappear after 24 hours.

How do I add someone? Circle → Invite Friends, pick a group (Friends to start), then share that group's code. They type it at the top of Invite Friends in MoodPals, and you tap Accept when the request shows up.

How do I make more groups? Free includes the Friends group. Create a Group opens MoodPals Plus, which adds unlimited groups. If you're using MoodPals as a guest, you'll be asked to create an account first.

How do I stop sharing with someone? Open their profile in My Circle to mute them, hide your moods from them or remove them.

MoodPals Plus

What does it cost? $1.99/month or $19.99/year in the US. Local prices are shown in the App Store.

Restore a purchase: More → Subscription → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

Cancel: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → MoodPals. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

Refunds: Apple handles payments. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Export or delete my data: email hello@moodpals.com from your account's email address (or include the guest ID shown in More → Settings). We'll confirm and complete your request within 30 days.

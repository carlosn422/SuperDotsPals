What MoodPals does

Daily stress score

Know your stress level in one glance

MoodPals compares each Apple Watch HRV reading with your own last 21 days, not population averages, and shows a clear level: Great, Normal, Elevated or Overload. You also get an energy score built from your sleep, stress and activity.

  • HRV trend coloured by stress level
  • Hourly heart rate and resting heart rate
  • Day, week, month and year stress trends
Body & recovery

Sleep, activity and calm, all in one place

Sleep duration and stages, Move/Exercise/Stand rings, daylight, mindful minutes, steps and noise levels come straight from Apple Health. One tap starts a guided breathing break when your pal looks worried.

  • Sleep detail with stages and heart rate while asleep
  • Calm, box and unwind breathing
  • Water, caffeine and mood check-ins
Stress diary

Find out what actually drives your stress

Every diary entry is matched to your stress level at the time you wrote it. Tag what's going on (work, sleep, caffeine…) and MoodPals shows which tags come up most often on your hardest days, plus a weekly recap.

  • Timeline coloured by stress level
  • "What drives your stress" insights
  • Prompts that change with how today is going
Ask MoodPal

An AI pal that knows your numbers

Ask "Why is my stress high today?" and get an answer based on your own readings and diary, with buttons to start a breathing break, log water or open your trends. Save useful answers to your diary.

  • Suggested questions for today
  • Private: your data stays in your account
  • Not a therapist and not a diagnosis, just a friendly pal
My Circle & Little Signals

Share how you feel with the people you trust

Invite close friends or family with your personal code. Nobody can find you by searching. After a check-in, choose who sees your mood, and they can answer with an emoji reaction or a Little Signal: a quick emoji hug, a bit of sunshine or a "you've got this". The Circle tab shows a badge when someone reacts, and your notifications list shows who reacted, with which emoji, to which mood. Shared moods disappear after 24 hours.

  • You share your mood, never your raw health data
  • Circle tab with an unread badge and a list of who reacted
  • Groups like Close Friends or Family, each with the moods they can see
  • Emoji-only reactions ❤️ 🤗 🫶 ☀️ 💪 🎉
  • Mute, hide your moods from, or remove anyone at any time
Apple Watch

Your pal, right on your wrist

The MoodPals watch app shows the same animated pal as your iPhone, changing with your mood and stress level. Add a MoodPals complication and your mood sits right next to the time, so you can see it without opening the app. Measure your stress on demand, check in with a tap and pick a watch theme that suits you.

Your Circle comes along too. A mood you pick on your watch is shared with the groups you've allowed to see it, and while the app is open, a friend's reaction floats gently over your pal. A small Circle button shows how many reactions you haven't seen yet.

  • Share your mood with your Circle from your wrist
  • Friends' reactions float by while the app is open
  • Mood, Mood & Stress and Stress score complications
  • Measure now: a fresh stress reading in about a minute
  • Tap to change your mood, guided breathing on your wrist
  • Same stress score on your iPhone and Apple Watch

Questions

Do I need an Apple Watch?

For the stress score, yes. HRV is recorded by Apple Watch. Without one, you can still use mood check-ins, the stress diary, breathing and Ask MoodPal.

When does MoodPals launch?

We're inviting early subscribers to a TestFlight beta first. Join the list and you'll be among the first to get an invite.

How is the stress score calculated?

Each HRV sample is compared with the median of your previous 21 days, adjusted by your resting heart rate. Lower HRV and higher resting heart rate than your usual push the score up.

Do I have to use the Bible verse feature?

No. Spiritual reading is completely optional. Open it when you'd like encouragement; everything else in MoodPals works without it.

Is it free?

Yes. MoodPals Free includes all six pals, mood check-ins, the daily stress score, the Apple Watch app and My Circle. MoodPals Plus adds advanced trends, extra customization, expanded circles and AI reflections for $1.99/month or $19.99/year.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start as a guest and even subscribe to Plus without one. Create an account when you want to sync across devices or use My Circle; your data and subscription come with you.

What do my friends see when I share?

Only the mood you choose to share (for example "Calm"), with the groups you pick. They never see your heart rate, HRV, sleep or diary. Shared moods disappear after 24 hours, and you can stop sharing with anyone at any time.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, your mood and wellness history, your Circle data and any family watch profiles you manage. Deleting your account doesn't cancel an Apple subscription, so cancel Plus in your Apple Account too.

How do I cancel Plus?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose MoodPals. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid the next charge.

How do I contact you?

Email hello@moodpals.com. You can also visit our Support page.

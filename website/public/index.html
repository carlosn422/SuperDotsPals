Questions

Is my pal a therapist?

No. Your MoodPal is a caring friend to talk to, not a therapist or a medical service. If you're in crisis in the US, call or text 988.

Are my calls recorded?

No. Audio is streamed to our AI provider only during the call and isn't stored by MoodPals. Only the diary text you keep and the call length are saved.

Is it free?

Yes. MoodPals Free includes all six pals, mood check-ins, the stress score, the Apple Watch app and My Circle. Talking with your pal is part of MoodPals Plus.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

Only for the stress score. Talking with your pal, the diary and mood check-ins work on iPhone alone.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to More → Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and its data.

How do I contact you?

Email hello@moodpals.com or visit our Support page.

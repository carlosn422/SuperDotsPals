How the score works

A stress score based on you, not averages

Each HRV reading is compared with the median of your own previous 21 days and adjusted by your resting heart rate. Lower HRV and a higher resting heart rate than usual push the score up. After about 3 days of readings, MoodPals knows what normal looks like for you.

  • Great: under 35
  • Normal: 35 to 59
  • Elevated: 60 to 79
  • Overload: 80 and up

Questions

How does the Apple Watch measure stress?

Apple Watch records heart rate variability (HRV) in the background and saves it to Apple Health. MoodPals reads those readings and compares each one with your own last 21 days, adjusted by your resting heart rate, to give you a stress level from Great to Overload.

Is the MoodPals stress tracker free?

Yes. The daily stress score, the Apple Watch app and the watch-face complications are free. MoodPals Plus ($1.99/month or $19.99/year) adds longer trends, Body & recovery, AI reflections and more.

Do I need an iPhone too?

Yes. MoodPals is an iPhone app with an Apple Watch app included. Your iPhone reads Apple Health, and the watch shows your pal, your stress bar and lets you measure on demand.

Will MoodPals tell me when my stress is high?

Yes. HRV alerts are on by default and arrive when your stress is well above your usual, never more than once every 3 hours. You can turn them off in the app.

Is MoodPals a medical device?

No. MoodPals is a wellness app. Stress scores are estimates based on your own readings, not medical measurements or diagnoses.

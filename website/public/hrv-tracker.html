Questions

What is HRV?

Heart rate variability (HRV) is the small variation in time between your heartbeats. A higher HRV than your usual often means your body is rested and recovered, while a lower HRV than your usual can be a sign of stress, poor sleep or illness.

What is a good HRV?

It depends on the person. HRV varies a lot with age, fitness and genetics, so MoodPals compares each reading with your own previous 21 days instead of a single target number.

How does Apple Watch measure HRV?

Apple Watch records HRV in the background throughout the day and saves it to Apple Health. In the MoodPals watch app you can also tap Measure now for a fresh reading in about a minute.

Is the HRV tracker free?

Yes. Your HRV, heart rate and daily stress level are free. MoodPals Plus adds longer trends and 30 days of history.

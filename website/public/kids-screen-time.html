Questions

Can my child unlock the apps?

When time is up, Apple Screen Time locks the apps you chose. You release the break or add time from your Apple Watch or in MoodPals on the iPhone.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. You can start and stop Screen Break on the iPhone. With an Apple Watch you can control it from your wrist while your child has the phone, and reminders go to your watch instead of their screen.

What happens if MoodPals is closed?

The timer keeps running. Apple Screen Time locks the apps at the end time even if MoodPals is closed, the phone is restarted or your watch is out of range.

What do Screen Time Insights show?

How long each child used the phone during their Screen Breaks and how much time you granted, by Day, Week, Month and Year.

How much does it cost?

Screen Break comes with a 14-day free trial, shared with Screen Time & Stress. After that it's part of MoodPals Plus for $12.99 a month, $39.99 a year or $6.99 a week. Unlocking and ending a break always stay free.
